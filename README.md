Two games you can play on your own computer!

1. **⛏️ Block World** — a Minecraft-style building game (`index.html`)
2. **🦜 ¡Hola Amigo! Spanish Adventure** — learn Spanish with sound and your microphone (`spanish.html`)

# 🦜 ¡Hola Amigo! — Spanish Adventure

A talking Spanish game for kids learning the basics! The game **speaks every
word out loud in Spanish**, and you can use the **microphone** to say the
words back and earn stars ⭐.

## 🎮 How to play

1. Open the file called **`spanish.html`** in **Google Chrome** (Chrome or
   Edge is needed for the microphone to work — the first time, click
   **Allow** when it asks to use your microphone).
2. Pick a mode:
   - **📖 Learn & Speak** — flip through picture cards. Tap 🔊 to hear the
     word in Spanish, then tap 🎤 and say it yourself. Say it right and you
     get a star and confetti! 🎉
   - **🎮 Listening Game** — the game says a Spanish word... can you tap the
     right picture? 6 questions per round!
3. Pick a category: **Greetings 👋, Numbers 🔢, Colors 🎨, Animals 🐶, Food 🍎**

Your stars save automatically, so keep collecting! ⭐

# ⛏️ Block World 🌳

A Minecraft-style game you can play on your own computer — no internet needed!

![Block World](https://img.shields.io/badge/made%20with-blocks-brightgreen)

## 🎮 How to play it on your computer

1. Download this whole folder to your computer
   (on GitHub, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**, then unzip it).
2. Find the file called **`index.html`**.
3. Double-click it — the game opens in your web browser!

That's it. No installing, no internet needed after you download it.

## 🕹️ Controls

| What you press | What happens |
|---|---|
| Move the mouse | Look around |
| **W A S D** (or arrow keys) | Walk |
| **SPACE** | Jump (and swim up in water!) |
| **Left click** | Break a block 👊 |
| **Right click** | Place a block 🧱 |
| **Keys 1–8** (or scroll wheel) | Choose which block to build with |
| **ESC** | Pause and see the menu |

## 🌍 Fun things to know

- Every world has hills, lakes, beaches, and trees.
- Your world **saves itself automatically** — close the game and your
  buildings will still be there next time!
- Want to start over? Click **"Make a brand new world"** on the menu.
- You can build with grass, dirt, stone, sand, wood, leaves, planks, and brick.

## 📁 What's in this folder

- `index.html` — the page you double-click to play Block World
- `game.js` — the Block World game code (the world, walking, building...)
- `three.min.js` — the 3D graphics library ([Three.js](https://threejs.org/))
- `spanish.html` — the Spanish learning game (everything in one file!)

Have fun building! 🏰
